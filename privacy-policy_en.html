CalAlarm Privacy Policy

This app does not collect, upload, or analyze any of your personal data. Your data belongs to you and stays only on your device.

Privacy Commitment

  • No registration or login — no account needed
  • No server — no server receives your data
  • No personal data collected — no name/phone/location
  • No data uploaded — data stays local
  • No third-party tracking — no ad/analytics SDK
  • No ads — zero ad monetization

Permissions

  • Alarm permission (NSAlarmKitUsageDescription): used for system-level AlarmKit scheduling so alarms ring even in silent and Do Not Disturb modes
  • File import (system Files app / Share sheet): when you choose a custom ringtone, the app reads the audio file you explicitly select and transcodes it locally into an alarm sound stored in the app's Library/Sounds sandbox. The audio stays only on your device, is never uploaded to any server, and is not used for any other purpose

Subscription & Payment

  • Payments are handled by the Apple App Store; we never see or store any payment information
  • Subscription is tied to your Apple ID; there is no separate account system
  • Monthly, quarterly, and yearly plans are available; the yearly plan includes a 14-day free trial, auto-renews after the trial, and can be cancelled anytime

App Group Sharing

  • Used only to share the next alarm time with the widget so the countdown can be shown on the Home Screen
  • Only the next alarm's trigger time is shared; no other data is included
  • Data is passed only within the device-local App Group container and is never transmitted externally or to any server

Data Storage

  • All alarm data is stored locally on the device (SwiftData)
  • Custom ringtones you import from the Files app or Share sheet are stored locally (Library/Sounds) and removed when the app is uninstalled
  • Uninstalling the app erases all local data

Third-Party Services

  • This app does not use any third-party analytics SDK
  • This app does not use any third-party advertising SDK
  • This app does not embed any third-party tracking component
