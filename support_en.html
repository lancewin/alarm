Support

Features

Alarm

  • System-level alarm scheduling based on AlarmKit, rings even in silent and Do Not Disturb modes
  • Manage multiple alarms: create, edit, delete, enable, disable
  • Repeat rules: once, daily (free); weekdays, weekends, custom weekdays, alt weeks, shift schedules, custom dates (subscription required)
  • Snooze: configurable 1-30 minute interval and maximum snooze count
  • Ringtone: 8 built-in ringtones + custom ringtones imported from the Files app or system Share sheet (mp3/m4a/wav/aiff/caf etc., auto-trimmed to a ≤30s loop)
  • Vibration: can be enabled alone or together with the ringtone

Shift System

  • Fixed cycle: loops by work days + rest days (e.g., alt weeks, 2-on-1-off)
  • Shift rotation: cycles through shifts in order (e.g., day → night → rest, or two early + two late then a day off)
  • Custom dates: pick every day you need to ring across a whole month at once, even with no pattern at all

Temporary Alarm

  • One-time reminder that is deleted automatically after ringing
  • Preset categories: meeting, flash sale, cooking, pickup, custom
  • Ideal for one-off scenarios without cluttering the regular alarm list

Widget

  • The Home Screen widget shows the next alarm time
  • Data is shared via App Group, viewable without opening the app

Membership

  • Extended repeat types: weekdays, weekends, custom weekdays, alt weeks, shift schedules, custom dates
  • "Once" and "Every day" are free for all users

Subscription Management

  • Monthly, quarterly, and yearly plans are available; the yearly plan includes a 14-day free trial, auto-renews after the trial, and can be cancelled anytime
  • Subscriptions auto-renew and can be cancelled anytime
  • Manage in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • Restore Purchases recovers already-purchased membership

FAQ

Q: Will the alarm ring in silent mode?

A: Yes. Based on AlarmKit system-level alarms, which ring normally in silent and Do Not Disturb modes.

Q: Will the alarm ring after the app is closed?

A: Yes. System-level alarms ring on time even if the app is completely closed.

Q: How does subscription auto-renew?

A: The subscription auto-renews within 24 hours before the period ends, charged via the payment method tied to your Apple ID. To avoid renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Q: How do I cancel a subscription?

A: Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, select this app and tap "Cancel Subscription". You keep membership benefits until the end of the current period.

Q: How do I restore purchases?

A: Tap "Restore Purchases" on the subscription page; the system verifies your Apple ID and restores purchased membership.

Q: How is the free trial calculated?

A: First-time yearly subscribers get a 14-day free trial. You can cancel anytime during the trial without being charged. After the trial, paid subscription begins automatically.

Q: Is my data uploaded to a server?

A: No. All alarm data is stored only on your device locally (SwiftData) and is never uploaded to any server.

Q: How do I import my own ringtone?

A: In "Choose Ringtone", tap "Import from Files" to pick audio from the Files app (mp3/m4a/wav/aiff/caf etc.). You can also use the system Share sheet and choose "Copy to CalAlarm" from another app. The audio is transcoded locally into a ≤30s looping ringtone stored on your device, so it still plays at alarm time even if the app is closed. DRM-protected Apple Music songs cannot be imported.

Q: How do I handle shift or irregular schedules?

A: In "Repeat Rule", choose "Shift schedule" or "Custom dates":
• Fixed cycle: loops by work N days + rest M days (e.g., alt weeks, 2-on-1-off);
• Shift rotation: cycles through shifts in order (e.g., day → night → rest), each shift with its own time and ringtone — ideal for early/late shifts or two early + two late then a day off;
• Custom dates: pick every day you need to ring across a whole month at once, even with no pattern at all. Perfect for nurses, drivers and shift workers who never miss or mis-set an alarm.
