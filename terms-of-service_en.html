2. App Description

This is an iOS AlarmKit-based smart alarm app that requires no registration or login and stores data locally.

3. Subscription Service

  • Monthly $0.99 / Quarterly $1.99 / Yearly $5.99
  • Auto-renewing, charged 24 hours before period ends
  • Cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • Free features (no subscription): unlimited alarms, default ringtones, custom ringtones (from music library), vibration, snooze, temporary alarms
  • Pro features (subscription required): extended repeat types — weekdays, weekends, custom weekdays, alt weeks, shift schedules, custom dates ("Once" and "Every day" are free for all users)
